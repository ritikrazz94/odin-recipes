Punjabi Sweet Lassi Recipe

Sweet Punjabi Lassi recipe this refreshing drink is a true summer favorite across India. Made using fresh curd (dahi) and sugar, this 2 ingredient lassi recipe is simple, quick, and incredibly delicious.

The creamy texture and naturally cooling properties make sweet Punjabi lassi perfect for beating the heat. Traditionally served chilled in earthen kulhads, it offers an authentic desi experience. You can enhance the flavor with cardamom powder, rose water, or a topping of dry fruits like almonds and pistachios. Whether enjoyed after meals or as a midday refreshment, dahi ki lassi is both satisfying and energizing.

ingredients

  • ice cubes
  • 1 3/4 cups plain yogurt
  • 1 1/2 cups ice water
  • 6 cubes ice, crushed
  • 2 teaspoons white sugar
  • 1 pinch salt

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Fill 6 tall glasses with ice cubes.
  3. Place yogurt, ice water, crushed ice, sugar, and salt in a blender; blend until frothy.
  4. Pour over ice cubes in the glasses to serve.
